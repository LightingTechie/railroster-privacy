RailRoster Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

RailRoster is a roster management app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use RailRoster.

1. Information Stored by RailRoster

RailRoster is designed to store the information you enter into the app locally on your device. This may include:

  • Roster dates and shift details
  • Duty numbers and book-on/book-off times
  • 7-day provisional and 48-hour final allocation information
  • Overtime details
  • Rest days worked and Sunday working
  • Shift swap information and notes
  • Annual leave dates and entitlement settings
  • Roster rotation settings

2. Information We Collect

RailRoster does not require you to create an account and does not collect or transmit the roster information you enter to a RailRoster server.

The app does not use advertising or third-party analytics services to track your use of the app.

RailRoster does not request or use your device location.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information you enter is used solely to provide the features of RailRoster, such as displaying your roster, calculating durations and overtime, maintaining your history, and showing annual leave and rotation information.

4. Sharing of Information

RailRoster does not sell, rent, or share the information you enter into the app with third parties.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Roster information is stored locally on your device. Because the app does not maintain a RailRoster account or central database containing your roster information, we do not have access to retrieve or delete that information remotely.

You can delete information within the app where deletion functionality is provided, or remove the app from your device. Removing the app may delete locally stored app data, subject to the behaviour of iOS and any device backup or restore features you use.

6. Children's Privacy

RailRoster is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, or delete personal information and to object to or restrict certain processing.

Because RailRoster is designed not to transmit the roster information you enter to us, we generally cannot access that information on your behalf. If you have a privacy question or believe we may hold personal information about you, please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to RailRoster or applicable privacy requirements. The latest version will be published on this page, with the date shown at the top.
